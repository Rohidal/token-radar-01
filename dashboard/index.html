The three factors

Multiple. Every protocol in the snapshot that records both a market cap and a TVL above $5M is a comparable: market cap over TVL is what the market pays for that business. A category with at least four comparables sets the multiple for pre-token protocols in it, blended 65/35 with the whole snapshot's median so a thin category cannot run away with a number.

Momentum. Seven-day TVL change, clamped to minus 60 and plus 120 per cent, counted at 35 per cent weight: capital arriving is a signal, a single week is not a valuation.

Track record. Days since DefiLlama began tracking the protocol, up to four years, mapped into a 0.75 to 1.20 factor. A protocol that has held TVL for years without a token has more to hand a holder and more reason to launch; one listed last month has not shown that it lasts.

implied = TVL × multiple × (1 + growth × 0.35) × record

What this board does not claim

A protocol that records no token may still have one its DefiLlama record does not carry, and no implied value here is a forecast of a launch or of a price. The figure is the entry's own claim from the snapshot's own numbers, stated so a reader can disagree with it line by line. The watchlist is the floor the house set, never the ceiling: rows below it are the early radar, ranked the same way.
